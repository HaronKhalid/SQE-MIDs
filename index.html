Chapter 1: Introduction to Software Quality Engineering

Quality · Quality Types · Quality Control · Different views of Quality

What is Quality?

Quality = meeting the customer's requirements, at the agreed cost, within the agreed time.
Also: “Fitness for purpose” (Joseph Juran) and customer satisfaction.
  • Three things together: requirements + cost + time.
  • A good product that comes late or costs too much is not quality.
اردو مثالآپ نے درزی سے کہا: "کل تک 2000 روپے میں کُرتا چاہیے۔" اگر کُرتا ٹھیک ناپ کا، 2000 میں اور کل تک مل گیا تو کوالٹی ہے۔ اگر خوبصورت ہے مگر ایک ہفتہ لیٹ آیا یا 3500 کا پڑا، تو کوالٹی نہیں۔

Definitions of Quality

WhoSimple meaning
American Heritage DictionaryA characteristic or attribute of something.
JuranFitness for purpose.
IEEE GlossaryDegree to which a system, component or process meets (1) specified requirements and (2) customer needs or expectations.
ISO 8402The totality of features of a product/service that bear on its ability to satisfy stated or implied needs.
Gerald Weinberg“Quality is value to some person.” So it is subjective: different people feel different quality in the same software.
اردو مثال (Weinberg)ایک ہی موبائل ایپ طالب علم کو بہت اچھی لگتی ہے کیونکہ مفت ہے، لیکن دکاندار کو بری لگتی ہے کیونکہ اس میں بل بنانے کی سہولت نہیں۔ کوالٹی "کسی شخص کے لیے قدر" ہے۔

Who said it?

Software Quality (Roger Pressman)

Software quality = conformance to (1) explicit functional & performance requirements, (2) documented development standards, and (3) implicit characteristics expected from all professional software.

3 important points:

  • Requirements are the foundation. No conformance to requirements = no quality.
  • Standards guide how software is built. If you ignore them, lack of quality is almost sure.
  • If software meets explicit requirements but fails implicit ones, quality is suspect.
اردو مثالواضح (explicit): "ایپ میں لاگ اِن ہو۔" غیر واضح (implicit): کسی نے کہا نہیں مگر ہر کوئی چاہتا ہے کہ ایپ بار بار کریش نہ ہو اور پاس ورڈ محفوظ رہے۔ اگر ایپ لاگ اِن کر دے مگر روز کریش ہو تو کوالٹی مشکوک ہے۔

Variation Control

Variation is the heart of quality control. Goal: reduce the difference between predicted resources (staff, equipment, time) and actual resources used.
اردو مثالآپ نے اندازہ لگایا کہ پروجیکٹ 3 ماہ اور 4 بندوں میں مکمل ہوگا۔ حقیقت میں 6 ماہ اور 7 بندے لگ گئے۔ یہ بڑا "فرق" (variation) ہے۔ اچھی ٹیم اس فرق کو کم سے کم رکھتی ہے۔

Quality Type 1: Quality of Design

How well the software is designed. It is the quality the producer intends to give the customer (keeping customer requirements in mind).
  • Design is only 5% of product cost but has 70% influence on quality, manufacturability, serviceability and market acceptance.
  • Improving quality of design also improves quality of conformance.
اردو مثالگھر کا نقشہ (design) بنانے پر کل خرچ کا بہت کم حصہ لگتا ہے، مگر اگر نقشہ ہی غلط ہو (ہوا اور روشنی کا انتظام نہ ہو) تو بعد میں مستری جتنا بھی اچھا کام کرے، گھر اچھا نہیں بنے گا۔

Quality Type 2: Quality of Conformance

How well the product made matches the design/specification. It focuses on implementation. If it matches fully, conformance is “excellent”.
  • Specification = targets (ideal values) + tolerances (acceptable deviation).
  • Exact target is hard to hit every time because of variation in material, machine, men and process (4 Ms).
اردو مثالسموسے کا ڈیزائن: وزن 100 گرام (target)، 5 گرام کم یا زیادہ چلے گا (tolerance)۔ اگر 97 گرام نکلا تو ٹھیک، 85 گرام نکلا تو conformance خراب۔

It is perception, not objective quality, that drives preference, satisfaction, loyalty, sales and profit. Example: it took Google 3 years after launch to be perceived as the superior search engine.
اردو مثالدو موبائل کی ریم، بیٹری اور کیمرا بالکل برابر ہیں (objective برابر)۔ مگر لوگ مشہور برانڈ کو "زیادہ اچھا" سمجھتے ہیں کیونکہ اشتہار اور دوستوں کی رائے ہے (perceived مختلف)۔

Objective or Perceived?

Research: How objective quality affects perceived quality (Mitra & Golder)

  • Studied 241 products, 46 categories, over 12 years.
  • A change in objective quality is fully reflected in perception only after about 6 years.
  • In the first year only about 20% of the total effect shows.
  • Effects are bigger and faster for a decrease in quality than for an equal increase (bad news travels fast).
  • High-reputation brands get a “double advantage”: rewarded 3 years quicker for an increase, punished 1 year slower for a decrease.
اردو مثالاگر کسی مشہور دکان کی مٹھائی ایک دن خراب نکل آئے تو لوگ کچھ عرصہ معاف کر دیتے ہیں۔ نئی دکان کی مٹھائی بہتر بھی کر دیں تو لوگ سال ہا سال یقین نہیں کرتے۔

Perception curve (illustration)

Shape is only illustrative: year 1 = 20%, an increase for a low-reputation brand completes in ~6 years, a high-reputation brand is 3 years quicker; a decrease is faster, but high reputation delays the punishment by 1 year.

Quality Engineering

The set of operational, managerial and engineering activities a company uses to make sure product quality characteristics are at the nominal (required) levels.
اردو مثالفیکٹری میں مشین چلانا (operational)، منیجر کا منصوبہ بنانا (managerial) اور انجینئر کا مشین ٹھیک کرنا (engineering): تینوں مل کر یقینی بناتے ہیں کہ بسکٹ ہر بار ایک جیسا بنے۔

Quality Control (QC)

QC = processes and methods used to monitor work and see whether requirements are met. It focuses on reviews and removal of defects before shipment.
  • Objective: minimize produced defects, increase product quality.
  • Done by the same team that builds the product, or by a separate QC group/department.
  • Implementation approaches: fully automated · entirely manual · combination of tools + humans.
اردو مثالبیکری میں ڈبل روٹی پیک ہونے سے پہلے ایک بندہ ہر روٹی دیکھتا ہے۔ جلی ہوئی نکال دیتا ہے۔ یہ QC ہے: ڈلیوری سے پہلے نقص ڈھونڈنا۔

A quality model is a set of characteristics and the relationships between them that gives the basis for specifying quality requirements and evaluating quality.

McCall's Factor–Criteria–Metrics Model

LevelPurposeView
Factorsto specifyExternal view (users)
Criteriato buildInternal view (developers)
Metricsto controlScale and method for measurement
  • Quality factor = behavioural characteristic of a system (correctness, reliability, efficiency, testability, portability, maintainability…).
  • Quality criterion = attribute of a factor related to development. Example: Modularity (cohesive components in one module) increases maintainability.
  • McCall model is aimed at system developers, used during development. It does not say much about functionality.
اردو مثالموٹر سائیکل: گاہک کہتا ہے "تیز اور کم پیٹرول پیئے" (factor، باہر کا نظارہ)۔ مکینک انجن کی ٹیوننگ اور وزن کم کرنا دیکھتا ہے (criteria، اندر کا نظارہ)۔ پھر کلومیٹر فی لیٹر ناپتا ہے (metric)۔

McCall's 11 Quality Factors

Product Revision(ability to change)
  • Maintainability — Can I fix it?
  • Flexibility — Can I change it?
  • Testability — Can I test it?
Product Transition(adapt to new environments)
  • Portability — use on another machine?
  • Reusability — reuse parts?
  • Interoperability — interface with another app?
Product Operation(how it works)
  • Correctness — does it do what I want?
  • Reliability — accurately all the time?
  • Efficiency — runs as well as it can?
  • Integrity — is it secure?
  • Usability — can I run it?

McCall's Quality Criteria and Relationships

CriterionMeaning
AccuracyPrecision of computations and outputs
CompletenessFull implementation of required functionality
Error ToleranceContinuity of operations under adverse conditions
ExpandabilityDegree to which functions can be expanded
Hardware IndependenceHow little the software depends on hardware
ModularityHighly independent modules
SimplicityEase of understanding the software
Relationships:
  • Each factor is positively influenced by a set of criteria, and the same criterion affects many factors. Example: Simplicity impacts reliability, usability and testability.
  • Improving one factor may hurt another. Example: portable code may be less efficient.
  • Some factors help each other. Example: improving correctness increases reliability.
اردو مثالکوڈ کو سادہ (simplicity) رکھنے سے بگ کم ہوتے ہیں (reliability)، نیا بندہ جلد سمجھتا ہے (usability for developers) اور ٹیسٹ لکھنا آسان ہوتا ہے (testability)۔ ایک چیز، تین فائدے۔

GQM: Goal – Question – Metric

GQM is an approach to software metrics with 3 levels:
1️⃣ Conceptual (Goal): list the major goals of the project/process.
2️⃣ Operational (Question): from each goal, questions to find out if it is being met.
3️⃣ Quantitative (Metric): what must be measured to answer the questions.
اردو مثالGoal: امتحان میں اچھے نمبر۔ Question: میں روز کتنی دیر پڑھتا ہوں؟ کتنے ٹاپک مکمل ہوئے؟ Metric: روزانہ گھنٹے، مکمل ٹاپک کی تعداد، پریکٹس ٹیسٹ کا اسکور۔

Chapter 3: Software Quality Engineering Cost

Cost of Quality · Prevention · Appraisal · Failure · 1-10-100 rule · Quality Cost Conformance model

Cost of Quality

Cost of quality = all costs spent to get quality or to do quality-related work. It is not only the price of making a good product; it is also the cost of NOT making a good product.
  • Every time work is redone, cost of quality goes up (reworking an item, retesting a component).
  • Any cost that would not exist if quality were perfect is part of the cost of quality.
  • Total quality cost = Prevention + Appraisal + Failure (Internal + External).
اردو مثال: بریانی کی دکان
  • Prevention: تازہ مصالحہ خریدنا اور باورچی کی ٹریننگ۔
  • Appraisal: دیگ کھولنے سے پہلے چکھ کر دیکھنا۔
  • Internal failure: چکھنے پر خراب نکلی تو دیگ پھینک دینا۔
  • External failure: گاہک نے شکایت کی، پیسے واپس کیے۔

Prevention Cost

Cost of activities that stop defects from happening. Cheapest way to manage quality: avoid defects in the first place.
  • Examples: quality planning, testing equipment, staff training, quality improvement process (e.g. Six Sigma).
اردو مثالموبائل کی دکان میں شروع سے ہی اچھا چارجر بیچنا اور عملے کو سمجھانا کہ کیسے استعمال کرنا ہے، تاکہ بعد میں شکایت ہی نہ آئے۔

Appraisal Cost

Cost of measuring, evaluating or auditing products to check they meet standards. Also called inspection cost. Done to catch defects before shipment.
  • Examples: inspections, testing, maintenance of test equipment, supervision of testing/inspection.
اردو مثالامتحان سے پہلے ٹیچر کا ٹیسٹ لینا تاکہ کمزوری پتہ چل جائے۔ ٹیسٹ پر وقت اور پیسہ لگتا ہے، یہ appraisal ہے۔

Internal failureExternal failure

اردو مثالدرزی نے کُرتا گاہک کو دینے سے پہلے دیکھا کہ آستین چھوٹی ہے، دوبارہ سلائی کی (internal)۔ اگر گاہک نے گھر جا کر پہنا تو واپس لے آیا اور سب کو بتایا (external)، یہ زیادہ مہنگا پڑتا ہے۔

Tangible vs Intangible Cost

TangibleIntangible
Quantifiable, tied to an identifiable source. Seen instantly (materials, wages, equipment rent).Not quantifiable. Not seen now, effects come later: lost productivity, lost customer goodwill, low morale, missed deadline, lost sales from bad reputation.

اردو مثالخراب کھانا پھینکنے کا نقصان (tangible) اندازہ ہو جاتا ہے۔ مگر جو گاہک ناراض ہو کر دوبارہ نہیں آیا اور دس لوگوں کو بتا دیا، اس کا نقصان گنا نہیں جا سکتا (intangible)، اور بہت بڑا ہوتا ہے۔

Cost of Good Quality vs Cost of Poor Quality

  • Cost of GOOD quality = Prevention + Appraisal (building systems and processes to reduce failure).
  • Cost of POOR quality = Internal failure + External failure + intangible costs (because quality was poor).

The 1-10-100 Rule

$1 spent on prevention saves $10 on correction and $100 on failure costs.
اردو مثالگھر بناتے وقت پائپ ٹھیک جوڑنے پر 1 روپیہ (روک تھام)۔ فرش بننے سے پہلے لیکیج پکڑی تو ٹھیک کرنے میں 10 روپے۔ فرش اور رنگ ہو جانے کے بعد پانی ٹپکا تو 100 روپے۔

Quality Cost Conformance Model (ECL)

  • An example of constrained optimization.
  • Prevention + appraisal costs rise as conformance level rises.
  • Failure costs fall as conformance level rises.
  • So total cost is U-shaped.
  • Economic Conformance Level (ECL) is reached where prevention + appraisal costs are equal to the internal + external failure costs (the lowest point of total).
اردو مثالکھانے میں 100٪ پرفیکٹ بنانے کے لیے بہت مہنگا سامان اور انسپکشن لگے گا، اتنا کہ منافع ہی ختم۔ اور بالکل چیک نہ کریں تو شکایتیں اور نقصان۔ درمیان کا ایک "عقل مند" مقام ہے جہاں کل خرچ سب سے کم ہو۔ یہی ECL ہے۔

Repair Cost of Defects

The later a defect is found, the more expensive it is to fix. A mistake in requirements that reaches the customer costs far more than one caught in the requirement review.

Chapter 4: Software Quality Assurance

Quality Assurance · Defect prevention · Defect reduction · (containment starts here, details in Ch 5)

Quality Assurance (QA)

QA = establishing a framework of organizational procedures and standards that lead to high quality software. “Assurance”: if processes are followed, management can be assured of product quality.
History:
  • 1916: first formal QA and control function at Bell Labs (manufacturing).
  • 1950s–60s: programmers controlled their own product quality.
  • 1970s: QA standards first introduced in military contract software development.
QC (Ch 1)QA (Ch 4)
Checks the product, removes defects before shipmentBuilds the process/framework so quality is assured
اردو مثالQC: بیکری میں تیار روٹی دیکھ کر جلی ہوئی نکالنا۔ QA: بیکری کے قواعد بنانا: صفائی کا نظام، آٹے کا معیار، تندور کا درجہ حرارت، تاکہ روٹی جلے ہی نہیں۔

Classification Scheme for QA (dealing with defects)

1. Defect PreventionStop faults from being injected
2. Defect ReductionDetect and remove faults (static + dynamic testing)
3. Defect ContainmentLimit damage of failures (Chapter 5)
اردو مثال: مچھرروک تھام: پانی جمع نہ ہونے دیں (مچھر پیدا ہی نہ ہوں)۔ کمی: جو مچھر موجود ہیں سپرے سے مار دیں۔ کنٹرول: مچھر دانی لگائیں تاکہ کاٹ بھی لیں تو نقصان کم ہو۔

Prevention, Reduction or Containment?

Defect Prevention

Prevent faults from being injected via error blocking or error source removal.
  • Error source removal: find the root causes behind missing/incorrect human actions and fix them (e.g. correct human misconceptions).
  • Focus is on people and their conceptual mistakes, which may lead to bad choice of methodology, language, algorithm or QA strategy → many fault injections.
  • 3 techniques: (1) Education and training, (2) Process conformance and standards enforcement, (3) Tools/technologies and techniques.
اردو مثالاگر ہر نیا پروگرامر array کے index میں غلطی کرتا ہے تو بگ ٹھیک کرتے رہنے کے بجائے ٹریننگ دیں تاکہ غلط فہمی ہی ختم ہو جائے، یہ "error source removal" ہے۔

Education and Training

Helps people control, manage and improve how they work and removes misconceptions. Focus on 4 areas:

AreaWhy
Product & domain knowledgeIf people don't know the application domain, wrong solutions get built.
Tools (development/testing)Tool knowledge helps build high-quality software.
General software development knowledgeE.g. weak requirements analysis → rework in design, coding, testing.
Development process knowledgeE.g. in incremental development, if people don't know how increments fit together, interface problems occur.
Trick: P-T-G-P = Product, Tools, General, Process.
اردو مثالجو پروگرامر بینکنگ نہیں جانتا وہ سود اور سروس چارج کے حساب میں غلطی کرے گا۔ پہلے اسے بینکنگ کی بنیادی باتیں سکھائیں۔

Process Standards and Tools / Technologies

  • Process conformance and standards enforcement: make people follow the agreed process and standards, so mistakes are blocked.
  • Appropriate methodologies reduce fault injection. Many problems of low-quality “fat software” can be solved by discipline and a return to essentials: “lean software”.
  • Specific tools help: e.g. a syntax-directed editor that automatically balances every “{” with “}” in C reduces syntax problems.
اردو مثالورڈ میں spell-check خود غلط الفاظ پر لال لکیر لگا دیتا ہے، اس لیے غلطی لکھی ہی نہیں جاتی۔ کوڈ ایڈیٹر بھی قوسین خود بند کر دیتا ہے۔

Defect Reduction: Static Testing → Inspection

Defect reduction = detect and remove faults after they have been injected. Two categories: Static testing (no execution) and Dynamic testing (execute the program).

Software Inspection = critical examination of software artifacts (code, design, test cases, requirements, test plans) by human inspectors to find and fix faults.

  • Critical reading and analysis of artifacts.
  • Done by multiple inspectors through some coordination process.
  • Faults are found directly by humans (individually or in group sessions).
  • Faults found must be removed and the removal verified.
  • Includes planning and follow-up activities.
اردو مثالاخبار چھپنے سے پہلے تین پروف ریڈر مضمون الگ الگ پڑھتے ہیں اور پھر ملتے ہیں، یوں غلطیاں چھاپہ خانے سے پہلے پکڑی جاتی ہیں۔ پروگرام چلانے کی ضرورت نہیں پڑی۔

Chapter 5: Software Defect Containment

Fault tolerance · Recovery · Diversity techniques · Decision mechanisms · Fault Tree Analysis

Defect Containment: Why do we need it?

Defect containment = containing failures to local areas and limiting the damage.
(1) Fault tolerance breaks the cause-effect link between faults and failures so local faults don't cause global failures. (2) Failure containment avoids catastrophic results (death, injury, severe damage).
  • Software defects cannot be fully removed because systems are large and complex.
  • Remaining faults may be triggered in rare conditions. It is unrealistic to write test cases for all conditions or inspect all scenarios.
  • So we tolerate those faults or contain the failures to reduce damage.
اردو مثالبجلی جانا ہم نہیں روک سکتے، اس لیے UPS لگا لیتے ہیں تاکہ جب بجلی جائے تو کمپیوٹر بند ہو کر کام ضائع نہ ہو۔ بجلی کی خرابی (fault) موجود ہے مگر نقصان (failure) نہیں ہوا۔

Software Fault Tolerance

Fault tolerance / graceful degradation = the system continues to operate properly when some components fail. If quality decreases, the decrease is proportional to the severity of the failure.
  • Assumes the system has unavoidable and undetectable faults and makes provisions to operate correctly anyway.
  • Faults are detected and recovery happens using various methods.
  • All fault tolerance is based on redundancy (for detection and recovery).
  • Stages: error detection → confinement → evaluation of propagation of erroneous info → recovery.
اردو مثال: گریس فل ڈیگریڈیشنچار انجن والا جہاز اگر ایک انجن کھو دے تو گرتا نہیں، بس رفتار تھوڑی کم ہو جاتی ہے۔ جتنی خرابی، اتنی ہی کمی۔

Error Detection

  • Starting point of fault tolerance: verification tests to detect errors.
  • Uses active redundancy: redundant elements operating simultaneously to prevent, or allow recovery from, failures → more availability.
  • Forms of redundancy: Hardware · Software · Time · Information.
ApproachHow it detects
ReplicationUses copies of the system, gives the same input, compares outputs. If results differ → error detected.
TemporalWatches an expected time interval. If the watch expires with no response → error detected (watchdog timer).
اردو مثالReplication: ایک ہی حساب دو بندوں سے کروائیں؛ جواب مختلف آئے تو غلطی۔ Temporal: امتحان میں گھنٹی؛ وقت ختم ہوا اور جواب نہیں آیا تو مسئلہ۔

Recovery Types

Backward recoveryForward recovery
Undoes executed actions and returns to an earlier consistent state, then continues from there.When an abnormal state is found, suspends processing until better conditions; ignores part/all of the work and continues with what is assumed correct.
Needs a recovery point that saves information during execution.Corrects the wrong behavior by sending compensation information.
اردو مثالپیچھے کی طرف: ویڈیو گیم میں آپ مرے تو آخری سیو پوائنٹ سے دوبارہ شروع (backward)۔ آگے کی طرف: بینک نے غلطی سے 500 زیادہ کاٹ لیے تو پرانا لین دین ختم کرنے کے بجائے اگلے ہی دن 500 واپس ڈال دیے (forward، compensation)۔

Fault Injection (for fault tolerance assessment)

  • Testing the software under abnormal circumstances with erroneous or invalid external inputs.
  • Goal: inject faults and see if the system can detect and recover as specified.
  • Results can lead to fixing individual bugs or discovering design deficiencies in fault tolerance.
اردو مثالفائر ڈرل: جان بوجھ کر الارم بجا کر دیکھنا کہ لوگ صحیح نکلتے ہیں یا نہیں۔ اسی طرح سافٹ ویئر میں جان بوجھ کر غلط ان پٹ یا نیٹ ورک بند کر کے دیکھتے ہیں کہ نظام کیسے سنبھالتا ہے۔

اردو مثالDesign diversity: ایک ہی سوال تین مختلف استادوں سے حل کروانا۔ Data diversity: ایک ہی استاد کو سوال تھوڑا مختلف انداز میں پوچھنا (مثلاً 0.5 کی جگہ 1/2)۔

Which technique?

Recovery Blocks (RB)

  • Introduced 1974 by Horning; early implementations by Randell (1975) and Hecht (1981).
  • Uses repeated execution (redundancy over time). Related to sequential systems.
  • Parts: a conventional block + acceptance test + primary alternate + zero or more stand-by spares.
  • If an alternate crashes (divide by zero, time limit exceeded) or fails the acceptance test, the state is restored to what it was before the primary started, then the next alternate runs.
ensure   acceptance test
by       primary alternate
else by  alternate 2
else by  alternate n
else     error
اردو مثالپلان A: موٹر سائیکل پر جانا۔ اگر پنکچر ہو (acceptance test fail) تو پہلے حالت واپس (سامان وہیں) اور پلان B: رکشہ۔ وہ بھی نہ ہو تو پلان C: بس۔ آخر میں سب fail تو "error"۔

Recovery Block simulator (task: double the number)

Acceptance test: result must be a number between 0 and 1000.

N-Version Programming (NVP)

  • Suggested by Elmendorf (1972), developed by Avizienis & Chen (1977–78).
  • Uses parallel redundancy: N different versions fulfilling the same function run in parallel; the voter (decision algorithm) compares all outputs and selects the correct one (usually majority vote).
  • Aim: mask the effect of software flaws in the module's output. Local failure in a few versions will not spoil the global result.
  • Compared with RB, NVP is a static technique. Voter is the fundamental difference between NVP and RB.
  • Needs lots of development effort and teams (high cost).
run Version 1, Version 2, ..., Version n
if (Decision Mechanism (Result1, Result2, ..., Result n))
     return Result
else failure exception
اردو مثالتین ڈاکٹروں سے الگ الگ رائے لیں۔ دو کہیں "ٹائیفائیڈ" اور ایک "ملیریا" تو اکثریت کی بات مانتے ہیں۔ ایک ڈاکٹر کی غلطی سے مریض کو نقصان نہیں ہوا۔

N-Self-Checking Programming (NSCP)

  • Uses multiple versions combined with structural variations of RB and NVP.
  • Versions and acceptance tests are developed independently from common requirements.
  • Main difference from RB: a separate acceptance test for each version.
  • Versions and tests can run sequentially or in parallel; the output is taken from the highest-ranking version that passes its own acceptance test.
اردو مثالتین طلبہ اپنا اپنا جواب لکھتے ہیں اور ہر کوئی اپنا جواب خود چیک بھی کرتا ہے۔ جو سب سے سینئر طالب علم کا جواب اس کی اپنی چیکنگ میں پاس ہو، وہی جمع ہوگا۔

NVP vs NSCP simulator

Retry Blocks (RtB)

  • By Ammann & Knight (1987–88). A modification of the recovery block that uses data diversity instead of design diversity.
  • Same algorithm, different data: if the first try fails the acceptance test, re-express the input (equivalent or nearly equivalent form) and run again.
  • Repeats until the test passes or the deadline expires; then a backup algorithm runs with the original input; if that fails → failure exception.
ensure   Acceptance Test
by       Primary Algorithm (Original Input)
else by  Primary Algorithm (Re-expressed Input)
else by  Primary Algorithm (Re-expressed Input)
... [Deadline Expires]
else by  Backup Algorithm (Original Input)
else     failure exception
اردو مثالکیلکولیٹر 0 پر فیل ہوتا ہے تو آپ 0 کی جگہ 0.0001 ڈال کر دوبارہ حساب لگاتے ہیں۔ جواب تقریباً وہی آتا ہے۔ طریقہ وہی، ڈیٹا ذرا بدلا ہوا۔

Retry Block simulator

Primary algorithm computes 100 ÷ x but has a hidden bug: it crashes whenever x is a multiple of 10. Re-expression adds a tiny change (x + 0.01 × attempt).

N-Copy Programming (NCP)

  • The data-diverse version of NVP.
  • Uses one or more Data Re-expression Algorithms (DRA) and at least two copies of the same program.
  • Inputs go through the DRAs → copies run in parallel on the re-expressed data → a Decision Mechanism (DM) picks the best result, if there is one.
  • Parts: executive, 1 to n DRAs, n copies, and a DM. The executive orchestrates the steps.
run DRA 1, DRA 2, ..., DRA n
run Copy 1(result of DRA 1), Copy 2(result of DRA 2), ..., Copy n(...)
if (Decision Mechanism (Result 1, Result 2, ..., Result n))
     return Result
(else error occurs)
اردو مثالایک ہی کاریگر کی تین نقلیں (copies)، ہر ایک کو ناپ تھوڑے مختلف انداز میں دیا (cm، انچ، گز)۔ آخر میں منیجر (DM) دیکھتا ہے کہ کس کا نتیجہ سب سے صحیح ہے۔
Design diversityData diversity
Sequential (try one by one)Recovery BlockRetry Block
Parallel (vote/decide)N-Version ProgrammingN-Copy Programming

Decision Mechanism

VoterCompares results from two or more variants and decides the correct result, if any exists.
ComparatorA voter when there are exactly two results.
Acceptance testChecks the final result. Doesn't matter which alternative produced it; only that it is within acceptable limits. Must be simple.
A badly designed acceptance test causes two flaws: (1) rejecting a correct alternative, (2) accepting an incorrect alternative.
اردو مثالٹیچر کا چیک: "جواب 0 سے 100 کے درمیان ہو" (آسان acceptance test)۔ اگر کوئی غلط جواب 40 لکھ دے تو بھی پاس ہو جائے گا (incorrect accepted)۔ اگر ٹیسٹ بہت سخت ہو تو صحیح جواب 101 بھی رد ہو سکتا ہے (correct rejected)۔

Failure Containment & Fault Tree Analysis (FTA)

Even with prevention and tolerance, faults remain. Can we (a) prevent accidents and (b) reduce their damage? We can't prevent all accidents, but we can analyze hazards and limit damage.
  • FTA was developed in 1962 at Bell Labs by H.A. Watson, under a US Air Force contract, to evaluate the Minuteman I ICBM launch control system.
  • FTA is a top-down, deductive failure analysis: start from an undesired state and use Boolean logic (AND, OR) to combine lower-level events.
  • Used in safety engineering and reliability engineering to find the probability of a safety accident or a system-level failure.
  • Steps: identify possible failures → list events that cause the accident → draw an upside-down tree (nodes = failures; edges = AND/OR).
  • Uses of the tree: accident elimination, reduction, control.
GateTop event happens when…Probability
ORany one child happens1 − (1−p1)(1−p2)…
ANDall children happenp1 × p2 × …
اردو مثال: گاڑی سٹارٹ نہیں ہو رہیٹاپ: "گاڑی سٹارٹ نہیں" ← OR (بیٹری ڈیڈ، یا پیٹرول ختم، یا سیلف خراب)۔ ہر وجہ کے نیچے اور وجوہات۔ ہمیں درخت سے پتہ چلتا ہے کہ سب سے آسان راستہ کون سا ہے جس سے حادثہ ہو سکتا ہے، پھر وہیں حفاظت لگاتے ہیں۔

One-Page Cheat Sheet

Ch 1: Quality basics

  • Quality = requirements + agreed cost + agreed time; fitness for purpose (Juran)
  • IEEE: meets specified requirements + user needs. ISO 8402: stated or implied needs. Weinberg: value to some person
  • Pressman: explicit req + standards + implicit characteristics
  • Variation control: predicted vs actual resources
  • Design (5% cost, 70% influence) vs Conformance (matches design; target ± tolerance)
  • Objective (measured) vs Perceived (subjective; drives sales; Google 3 yrs)
  • Mitra & Golder: 241 products, 46 categories, 12 yrs; 20% in year 1; full ~6 yrs; drops faster; high brand: +3 yrs quicker, −1 yr slower
  • QC: monitor work, remove defects before shipment; automated/manual/both

Ch 2: Models

  • McCall 11/23: factors (specify, external) · criteria (build, internal) · metrics (control)
  • Revision: Maintainability, Flexibility, Testability
  • Transition: Portability, Reusability, Interoperability
  • Operation: Correctness, Reliability, Efficiency, Integrity, Usability
  • Trade-offs: integrity↔efficiency (inverse), usability↔efficiency (inverse), maintainability↔flexibility (direct)
  • ISO 9126 (6/27): Functionality, Reliability, Usability, Efficiency, Maintainability, Portability
  • Boehm 1978 (7/15): as-is utility, maintainability, portability
  • FURPS (Grady, HP, 1987; IBM FURPS+): Functionality, Usability, Reliability, Performance, Supportability + design, implementation, interface, physical
  • GQM: Goal (conceptual) → Question (operational) → Metric (quantitative)

Ch 3: Cost

  • Total = Prevention + Appraisal + Failure (internal + external)
  • Prevention: planning, training, Six Sigma. Appraisal: inspection, testing, test equipment
  • Internal: scrap, rework, repair, retest, rejects. External: warranty, returns, help line, legal
  • Intangible (image, goodwill) can be 3–4× tangible
  • Good quality cost = prevention + appraisal; poor = failures + intangible
  • 1-10-100: $1 prevent, $10 correct, $100 failure
  • ECL: total cost is U-shaped; optimum at the lowest point

Ch 4: QA

  • QA = framework of procedures and standards. 1916 Bell Labs; 50s–60s programmers; 70s military
  • Prevention → Reduction → Containment
  • Prevention: error blocking / error source removal; training (product, tools, general, process), standards, tools (lean software, syntax-directed editor)
  • Reduction: Static (inspection, walkthrough, desk check) · Dynamic (black-box, white-box)
  • Inspection: multiple human inspectors + planning + follow-up. Desk check: one-person
  • Black-box: external functions vs spec. White-box: internal structure

Ch 5: Containment

  • Defects can't be fully removed → tolerate faults, contain failures
  • Fault tolerance = graceful degradation, based on redundancy (hardware, software, time, information)
  • Stages: detection → confinement → propagation evaluation → recovery
  • Detection: replication (compare outputs), temporal (timer)
  • Recovery: backward (recovery point, undo) · forward (continue, compensation)
  • Fault injection: test under abnormal input
  • Design diversity (costly): Recovery Block (1974 Horning; AT + state restore) · NVP (Elmendorf 1972; voter; static) · NSCP (own AT per version)
  • Data diversity (cheaper, one version): Retry Block (Ammann & Knight; re-express input, deadline, backup) · NCP (DRA + copies + DM)
  • AT flaws: reject correct / accept incorrect
  • FTA: 1962 Bell Labs, H.A. Watson, Minuteman I; top-down, Boolean AND/OR; eliminate/reduce/control accidents
